## Change under test

The generic hung-task detector now recognizes four exact Qualcomm/Oplus
service-thread idle states before the Android vendor hook reports them:

| Thread | Accepted idle wait channel |
|---|---|
| `adci_thread` | `__scm_smc_call` or `wait_to_die` |
| `zram_comp` | `zram_qpace_comp` |
| `hfi_core_dbg_cl` | `hfi_core_dbg_listener` |
| `msm_hw_fence_so` | `hw_fence_soccp_listener` |

Every exception also requires `TASK_UNINTERRUPTIBLE`, `PF_KTHREAD`, and
`in_iowait == 0`. The exact task name and saved wait-channel symbol must both
match. Active Secure World, QPACE consumption, HFI transaction, and hardware
fence processing paths use different wait channels and remain visible to the
hung-task detector. The detector, Oplus enhancement, and 60-second timeout
remain enabled.

## Physical canary

The exact release boot image passed:

- full active-boot write and exact read-back SHA-256;
- Android boot with `sys.boot_completed=1`;
- user0 `RUNNING_UNLOCKED`;
- F2FS `/data` with `inlinecrypt`;
- stock module population and Rust Binder load;
- Wi-Fi connectivity and external ping;
- Bluetooth initialization;
- NFC initialization;
- LTE/RMNET data initialization;
- USB/ADB;
- ZRAM and CPUFreq operation;
- empty pstore fatal-record scan.

The device was monitored through 692 seconds after boot. There were zero
target hung-task reports at or beyond the previously observed approximately
121-, 183-, 429-, and 490-second windows. There were also zero panic, Oops,
BUG, soft-lockup, RCU-stall, blocked-task, fatal module-loader, or DRM/GEM
reports in the captured test window.

Existing platform diagnostics unrelated to this change remained visible,
including IRQ-enable, `proc_register`, touch `frame_put`, and eBPF warnings.
USB-disconnected deep suspend was not exercised because the device remained
connected for logging. Camera, audio, and fingerprint service presence was
observed, but those three functions were not directly exercised during this
specific watchdog canary.
