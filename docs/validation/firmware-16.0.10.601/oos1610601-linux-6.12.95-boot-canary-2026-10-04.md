## Known observations and remaining gates

The Oplus enhanced hung-task monitor reported `adci_thread`, `zram_comp`, and
`hfi_core_dbg_cl`. Their wait functions and call paths match the physically
good 6.12.91 baseline, so they are recorded as inherited OOS behavior rather
than a 6.12.95 regression. No associated functional failure was observed.

USB-disconnected deep suspend was not tested. Audio playback and fingerprint
authentication require direct owner confirmation; this session established
service/HAL health only. These remaining gates prevent classification as a
fully qualified stable baseline.
